If `grep -r` has ever taken seconds, or you have opened a search engine because you
could not remember a `find` flag, this category pays for itself immediately. The tools
here do the same job as the classics, but with far more sensible defaults, much better
speed, and output you can actually read. Three more go further than the classics ever did:
searching code by its structure rather than its characters, and two ways to pull readable
Markdown out of a PDF.
