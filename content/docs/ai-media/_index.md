Every project eventually needs assets it has no budget for: a hero image for the landing
page, a screenshot with the clutter removed, a thumbnail at twice the resolution you
saved it at, a voice-over for a demo. None of that is your job, and all of it lands on
your desk anyway.

These five sites cover that work from a browser tab. No installs, nothing to configure —
open, drop a file in, get a result.

| Site | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Raphael](/docs/ai-media/raphael/) | Generating an image from a text prompt, free, without an account |
| [Krea](/docs/ai-media/krea/) | Drawing a rough shape and watching it become the image, live |
| [Magnific](/docs/ai-media/magnific/) | Enlarging an image and inventing the detail that wasn't there |
| [Clipdrop](/docs/ai-media/clipdrop/) | One-click background removal, object removal, relighting |
| [ElevenLabs](/docs/ai-media/elevenlabs/) | Turning a script into speech, or cloning a voice |

## Two things to settle before you publish anything

**Check what you're allowed to do with the output.** Free tiers frequently mean personal
use only, or a watermark, or attribution — and "I generated it" is not the same as "I may
sell it." Each page below states what the tier actually grants, but terms on tools this
young change faster than documentation does. Read the current ones before commercial use.

**Free tiers move.** Every service here has re-tiered at least once. Take the numbers on
these pages as the shape of the offer, not a quotation.
