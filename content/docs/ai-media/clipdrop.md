[Clipdrop](https://clipdrop.co/) is the utility drawer of this category. Not a creative
tool with a canvas and a learning curve — a page per job, where you drop an image in and
get it back fixed. Built at Stability AI and now run by Jasper, it has stayed exactly as
plain as it needs to be.

The tools you'll return to:

| Tool | Job |
|---|---|
| **Remove Background** | Cut the subject out, transparent PNG back |
| **Cleanup** | Brush over an object, person, or blemish; it fills the gap |
| **Text Remover** | Strip text and captions from an image |
| **Relight** | Add or move light sources on a finished photo |
| **Uncrop** | Extend the frame outward and invent what's beyond it |
| **Image Upscaler** | 2× on the free tier, more on paid |

Free use is a daily allowance per tool — roughly twenty runs each — at web resolution.
Paid raises the ceilings and unlocks high-resolution output. For occasional work you will
never reach the limit.

## The three that solve real problems

**Cleanup on screenshots.** A stray tooltip, a colleague's name in a sidebar, a
notification that shouldn't be in the documentation — brush it out, keep the background.
Faster than reproducing the screenshot, and it beats a black rectangle in a blog post.

**Remove Background for anything that needs to sit on a page.** Team photos, product
shots, an icon someone only has as a JPEG. Zoom in on hair and glasses before you ship it;
that's where cut-outs give themselves away.

**Relight when the photo is fine but the lighting isn't.** The usual save for a product
shot taken on a desk under an office ceiling.

## Where the seams show

- **Cleanup guesses.** Over a busy or structured background — brickwork, text, a repeating
  pattern — it invents something that doesn't line up. Work in small strokes and undo
  early rather than brushing over the whole region at once.
- **Uncrop is invention, not recovery.** It's making up the rest of the room. Fine for a
  background; not fine when the extended area implies something factual.
- **Free output is web resolution.** Check the pixel dimensions before sending a file to
  print.

## One thing to be careful about

Removing a watermark, a credit line, or a photographer's signature is exactly the kind of
job Text Remover does well — and doing it doesn't give you the right to use the image.
Same for cleaning something out of a photo that's presented as a record. The tool doesn't
know the difference; you do.
